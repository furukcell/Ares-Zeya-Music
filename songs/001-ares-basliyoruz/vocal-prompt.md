# Ares — Başlıyoruz | Vocal Prompt

## Vocal Identity

Use the canonical Ares Vocal DNA.

Young adult Turkish male voice, approximately 25–28 age impression, warm and slightly textured tone, medium-low register without an exaggerated deep voice, confident and relaxed delivery.

The voice must feel like the same Ares defined in music/vocal-dna.md.

## Verse Delivery

Conversational melodic rap.

- Short rhythmic phrases
- Clear Turkish pronunciation
- Relaxed confidence
- Tight rhythm
- Small pauses between phrases
- Occasional internal rhyme
- No shouting
- No exaggerated aggression

The delivery should sound like Ares is talking to himself while driving at night.

## Chorus Delivery

The chorus becomes noticeably more melodic.

Stretch the words:

**“Başlıyoruz”**

and

**“bitmedi”**

slightly.

The chorus should be memorable and easy to sing along with.

Increase emotional intensity without becoming theatrical.

## Vocal Processing

- Modern clean vocal
- Light controlled autotune
- Moderate compression
- Short/medium reverb
- Subtle delay on selected hook words
- Controlled low-end
- Vocal clearly in front of the instrumental

## Ad-libs

Use sparingly.

Possible short ad-libs:

- “yeah”
- “hadi”
- “tamam”
- short breath/vocal responses

Do not fill every gap with ad-libs.

## Performance Direction

The emotional arc is:

**alone → remembering → deciding → confidence → beginning**

The final chorus should feel bigger than the first chorus.

Do not imitate or reference any real artist's voice.
